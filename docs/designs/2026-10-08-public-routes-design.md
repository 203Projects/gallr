## Problem Statement

089 lets an author compose, save and share a route, but a shared route is reachable only by its link and is listed only to its author (MY tab 동선 section, Map tab route sheet). The owner wants app users to find routes other people made, with the most-saved routes promoted, and was unsure which screen should hold them.

This reverses the 089 office-hours deferral of a routes feed (decision "Personal routes v1 wedge", 2026-10-07), which waited for evidence that strangers open shared routes. The reversal is deliberate and logged (decision 9270cc61).

## Demand Evidence

None from users yet. The owner's reason is strategic: "Growth/retention bet". No one has asked to browse other people's routes, and 089 has not shipped, so its recipient-loop metric (`personal_route_recipient_loop`) has no data. The design therefore seeds supply with editors and measures whether anyone saves a route someone else made before adding likes, profiles or notifications.

## Status Quo

Today a visitor planning a gallery day uses the Map tab planner's generated routes (동네, 내 취향, 곧 종료, 저장한 전시) or builds their own route in the 089 composer. Routes made by editors or other users never reach them except through a link someone sends.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

- Reader: a gallr visitor in the Map tab route sheet planning a day out, who would rather start from a route someone else walked than build one.
- Supplier: gallr editors first (active `content.editor_memberships`), then signed-in authors who choose to list a saved route.
- Wedge: editor-seeded, opt-in user listing, ranked by saves, with staff pre-approval (Approach C).

## Constraints

- `DESIGN.md` binds every surface: monochrome, 0dp corners, one accent in three roles, Inter and Gothic A1, 8pt grid, inline bilingual copy.
- Shared-first architecture and TDD per the constitution: ranking, listing state and save rules live in `shared/commonMain` with tests first.
- Account and access: listing and approval run under the existing identity plane; editor status comes from `content.editor_memberships`, staff authority from the staff membership, never from client claims (`docs/account-identity-and-access.md`).
- Staging and production stay separate; migrations follow the recorded lineage.

## Premises (agreed)

1. Listing is opt-in and separate from link sharing. Routes already shared stay unlisted; their authors agreed to "anyone with the link", not to an in-app list showing their name.
2. Save is the only ranking signal in this slice. A save ("내 동선으로 복사") fills the saver's draft with the route, which becomes a 내 동선 route when they save it in the composer. No likes until saves show people browse.
3. Only live routes are listed: every stop still running or upcoming. Exhibitions close, so a route's freshness is built in and no arbitrary time decay is needed; live routes rank by saves in the last 30 days.
4. Editors publish at least 10 routes before launch; they carry an 에디터 label and share the same ranking.
5. Listed routes can be reported; staff can unlist them in Admin, reusing 089's revoke tooling.
6. It lives in the Map tab route sheet (owner's choice over the recommended 추천 strip), with a trigger to add a 추천 strip if saves stay low.
7. It ships as its own feature (090) after 089 is released, building on 089's tables.

## Approaches Considered

- A: Opt-in listing, public at once, report-based moderation. Ruled out: one poor route can hold a top spot until someone reports it.
- B: Full build with Admin editor authoring on the web, a review queue, author save notifications and save counts on the web page. Ruled out: a second composer and notifications before any save data exists.
- Placement alternatives ruled out: a 추천 tab strip plus list (recommended for visibility; owner chose the Map sheet), and a fifth bottom tab (changes the four-tab bar for an unproven feature).

## Recommended Approach: C, opt-in listing with staff pre-approval

Owner-approved approach (D9). Listing requests go through a short Admin review; editor routes skip it.

### Listing lifecycle

- Listing requires a published route (089's link share). The control lives on the saved route's row in 내 동선 (eng review D9): its ⋯ menu offers "공개 목록에 올리기" / "목록에서 내리기", or "공개하고 목록에 올리기" for a saved but unpublished route (publish, then request listing), and the row label shows the listing state. The composer is unchanged. A consent dialog (DD17) states what becomes visible: the route, its stops and the author's display name, in the app's public list.
- Who can read: anyone, signed in or not, can read the public list and open a listed route's preview, matching 089's public route page. Copying a route and reporting need sign-in.
- `listing_state` holds the author and staff decisions; whether a route actually shows is derived (see Ranking and Reports).

| State | Author sees | Shown in list |
|---|---|---|
| `unlisted` | 목록 미등록 (DD13) | no |
| `requested` | 목록 검토 중 | no |
| `approved` | 목록 승인됨, plus a DD13 line-3 reason when not eligible | yes, if eligible |
| `declined` | 목록 반려됨, reason on line 3 (DD16) | no |
| `removed` | 목록에서 내려짐 | no |

| From | Event | To |
|---|---|---|
| `unlisted` | author requests listing | `requested` (editor author: `approved`) |
| `requested` | author withdraws | `unlisted` |
| `requested` | staff approves / declines | `approved` / `declined` |
| `declined` | author edits and requests again (no limit) | `requested` |
| `approved` | author saves a change to the name or the stops | `requested` (editor author: `approved`); the route leaves the list until approved |
| `approved` | author turns listing off or deletes the route; staff revoke (089) | `unlisted` |
| `requested`, `approved`, `declined` | staff unlists | `removed` |
| `removed` | staff restores | `unlisted`; only staff can leave `removed`, and editor auto-approval never applies to it |

- An ended stop does not change the state: the route stays `approved` but ineligible, and the author sees "종료된 전시가 있어 공개 목록에서 빠졌어요". Removing the ended stop is a change to the stops, so it goes back to `requested` for review like any other edit.
- The author's display name is shown live, not snapshotted; a display name that breaks the rules is handled by staff unlisting (`removed`).
- Staff unlisting is separate from revoke: unlisting removes it from the list and keeps the link working; revoke (089) does both.

### Saving

- The preview's button is "내 동선으로 복사" (the same wording as the planner's copy action). It needs sign-in: a signed-out reader gets 089's sign-in request and the copy happens after sign-in, or not at all if they back out.
- Order (eng review D12): the existing replace-draft confirm first when the draft has unsaved stops (declining cancels everything); then `save_public_route`, which inserts one `route_saves` row (unique on route and account, so repeat taps never count twice); on success `copyIntoDraft(name, stops, expectedDraftId, expectedRevision)` writes the copy only if the draft is still the one confirmed, with a new route id and the route's name (eng review D16), and the composer opens; if the draft changed meanwhile, the preview shows "초안이 바뀌었어요 · 다시 시도" (a retry can't double-count). On a network failure the draft is untouched and the preview shows "복사하지 못했어요 · 다시 시도"; if the route is no longer listed or eligible it shows "이 동선은 더 이상 공개 목록에 없어요" and the list reloads.
- The copy is a new, ordinary unsaved draft (no provenance field, eng review D10; `route_saves` records who copied what); it becomes a saved route only when the saver saves it in the composer. Edits never touch the original. The save is counted at the tap, not at the later composer save.
- Copying your own route does not count.

### Ranking

- Eligible: `approved`, published, not revoked, and every stop running today or upcoming in Asia/Seoul (same `exhibitionStatus` rule the catalogue uses). A route with any ended stop is ineligible, because a route the reader can't complete misleads them. A route is also ineligible while any stop's exhibition is absent from `exhibition_catalog_v2` (owner-hidden, unpublished or deleted); its author sees "볼 수 없는 전시가 있어 공개 목록에서 빠졌어요" (eng review D7).
- Shared visiting day (eng review D15): a route is eligible only when `max(today, latest opening_date) <= earliest closing_date` in Seoul dates, so every stop is running on at least one common day; when that first shared day is after today, the row and preview show "{M}월 {D}일부터".
- Order: count of saves made on the current approved version (`approved_at = listing_decided_at`, D17) in the last 30 days, ties broken by newer approval. Computed only in the database (eng review D6): `list_public_routes(p_limit)` returns at most `p_limit` eligible routes in order; `content_private.list_public_routes_impl(p_limit, p_today)` takes the date so pgTAP can fix it. The app shows the order it receives; Kotlin holds row labels only.
- A route drops out the day a stop ends; the author sees "종료된 전시가 있어 공개 목록에서 빠졌어요" with a way to edit and resubmit.

### Where it lives (Map tab route sheet)

- A "추천 동선" section in the route sheet, below 내 동선 and above the curation modes: the top 3 ranked rows with an inline "동선 더 보기" expanding to the rest of the 10-row fetch (design review DD2, refining eng review D1; no separate list screen or paging), heading and order note per DD6, rows per DD5.
- Tapping a row opens a read-only route preview: the 089 route map, stop list with today's status lines, and "내 동선으로 복사". No editing in preview.
- Empty state (no approved live routes): the section is hidden rather than showing an empty list. Load failure (eng review D11): the 추천 동선 header shows GallrErrorMessage "추천 동선을 불러오지 못했어요" with 다시 시도, as 내 동선 does, and logs warn `public_routes_load_failed` through `AppLog.tagged("PublicRoutes")` with the exception type only.
- Move trigger (eng review D18, replacing the average-saves rule of decision 9270cc61): a `public_routes_viewed` mobile analytics event (dim `rows_shown` 1-10, no route ids) fires when the section is shown. From launch to week 4, compare section views per route-sheet open (exposure) with counted saves ÷ section views (interest). Low exposure → add a strip on 추천; healthy exposure with a low copy rate → no new surface. The spec sets the thresholds; reading it needs the 072 analytics activation.

### Admin

- The 089 route moderation workspace gains a queue of 검토 중 routes (oldest first) with approve and decline (reason chosen from a short list), plus a filter for reported routes and an unlist action.
- A decision applies only to the version staff reviewed (eng review D14): `decide_route_listing` takes the revision Admin displayed and raises `route_listing_stale` if `updated_at` changed; Admin reloads the route with "내용이 바뀌었어요 · 다시 확인해 주세요".
- Reports: a "신고" action in the preview's ⋯ menu with a reason. Reporting needs sign-in; an account has at most one open report per route (`route_reports` unique on route and account while unresolved); authors can't report their own routes.
- Reports never hide a route automatically (eng review D2): they wait in the Admin queue with their reasons. Staff dismiss them (the route stays listed) or uphold them, which unlists the route (`removed`).

### Data (sketch for the plan, not final)

- `personal_routes` gains `listing_state` (`unlisted`, `requested`, `approved`, `declined`, `removed`), `listing_requested_at`, `listing_decided_at`, `listing_decided_by`, `listing_decline_reason`.
- `route_saves (route_id, account_id, approved_at, created_at)` unique on (route_id, account_id, approved_at), where `approved_at` is the route's `listing_decided_at` when saved, so saves belong to the approved version (eng review D17); `route_reports (route_id, account_id, reason, created_at, resolved_at, resolution)` with a partial unique index on route and account where unresolved.
- RPCs: `request_route_listing`, `withdraw_route_listing`, `decide_route_listing`, `unlist_route` and `restore_route_listing` (staff), `save_public_route`, `report_route`, `resolve_route_reports` (staff), and `list_public_routes(p_limit)` returning at most `p_limit` (10) eligible routes ranked as above with the editor label (D1, D6). The list and preview reads are open to `anon` through these functions, like the 089 route page after D4; the others require an authenticated account, and the staff ones the staff membership.

## Success Criteria

- Within 4 weeks of launch: at least 30 approved live routes, at least 10 of them from users.
- Exposure and interest over launch to week 4, measured as in the move trigger (D18): section views per route-sheet open and counted saves ÷ section views, against thresholds set in the spec.
- Review turnaround: median under 24 hours from request to decision.
- Likes, author profiles and notifications are considered only after the save criteria are met.
- Stop rule (eng review D20): at week 4, if exposure is healthy but the copy rate is below the spec's threshold, stop expanding (no 추천 strip, likes, author profiles or notifications), editors stop seeding new routes, and the owner decides whether the section stays.

## Dependencies

- 089 released (migrations, `mobile-analytics`, Admin, web, then mobile), and its staging-only tasks (T043, T059, T063, T072) done.
- Editor accounts able to sign in to the app with an active `content.editor_memberships` row.
- Analytics events (author loop pipeline from 089) for `route_listing_requested`, `public_route_viewed`, `public_route_saved`, so the move trigger can be measured.
