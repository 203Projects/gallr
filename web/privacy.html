01 / OVERVIEW

About this policy

gallr ("we", "our", "us") operates the gallr mobile application (the "Service"). This policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose personal data when you use the Service.

02 / COLLECTION

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us and the limited information needed to operate the features you choose. Optional mobile usage analytics is a separate, default-off category described below.

  • Account and profileIf you create an account, we process your email address, authentication identifier, and the display name, biography, or profile photo you choose to provide.
  • Your gallr contentBookmarks, recorded visits, followed galleries, and thoughts may be stored on your device and synced to your account when you use account sync.
  • Gallery alertsIf you enable alerts, we process a random app-installation identifier, push address, platform, app version, language, and the galleries you follow so Apple or Google can deliver the alerts you requested. These identifiers are not reused for usage analytics.
  • Optional product interactionsLimited screen, exhibition, action, recommendation, and route bands described below, only when optional Usage analytics is on.
  • Location dataYour device processes location only when you grant permission to center the map and show nearby exhibitions. Precise location is not included in usage analytics.

Your choice / 선택

You can turn Usage analytics on or off at any time in Settings → Usage analytics. Turning it off immediately pauses future collection and clears unsent events; if clearing fails, the app keeps collection paused and offers a retry. Because previously accepted counts contain no account or reusable device identity, gallr cannot identify and remove one person's contribution from those aggregate counts.

설정 → 사용 분석에서 언제든 켜거나 끌 수 있습니다. 끄면 이후 수집을 즉시 중단하고 미전송 이벤트를 삭제하며, 삭제에 실패해도 수집은 중지된 상태로 유지되고 재시도할 수 있습니다. 이미 수락된 집계 수치에는 계정 또는 재사용 가능한 기기 식별자가 없으므로, 그 안에서 특정 개인의 기여분을 찾아 삭제할 수 없습니다.

04 / USE

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • Personalise your experience on your device and show relevant exhibitions.
  • Analyse optional, identity-free aggregate usage counts to improve the app.

05 / SHARING

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information or share mobile analytics with advertisers, data brokers, or paid-promotion systems. Supabase processes account, synced-content, alert, and analytics data as a service provider operating gallr's first-party infrastructure under our instructions. Apple and Google process push addresses only to deliver notifications you request. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the Service and its users.

06 / RETENTION & SECURITY

How long we keep data

We retain account, synced-content, and alert data only for as long as needed to provide the features you use and comply with legal obligations. You may delete your account in the app or request deletion of data linked to you by contacting us. Optional analytics follows the seven-day deduplication, 24-hour rate-limit, 24-calendar-month reporting, and hourly cleanup windows described above.

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

07 / CHILDREN

Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
