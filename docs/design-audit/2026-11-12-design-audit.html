Hangul may not render in Gothic A1

Verify, then fix code or doc. Seven unused fonts also ship (A29).

5 · Wording for the publish label

The private 메모 becomes a public 감상 on the exhibition page with the author's name, after a check. House tone: plain 해요체, quiet; no AI wording, no emoji, no review jargon for readers. "감상으로 공개" names a category ("as a 감상") instead of saying what happens, and the field above it is called 메모.
