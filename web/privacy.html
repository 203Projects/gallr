01 / OVERVIEW

About this policy

gallr ("we", "our", "us") operates the gallr mobile application (the "Service"). This policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose personal data when you use the Service.

02 / COLLECTION

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us and information generated automatically when you use the Service, including:

  • Device informationDevice type and operating system version.
  • Usage dataExhibitions viewed, tabs accessed, and bookmarks saved.
  • Location dataOnly when you grant permission, to show nearby exhibitions.

03 / USE

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • Personalise your experience and show relevant exhibitions.
  • Monitor and analyse usage patterns to improve the app.

04 / SHARING

Data sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties except as described in this policy. We may share information with service providers who assist us in operating the Service, subject to confidentiality agreements.

05 / RETENTION & SECURITY

How long we keep data

We retain your data for as long as necessary to provide the Service and comply with our legal obligations. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

06 / CHILDREN

Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

07 / CHANGES

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date above.
